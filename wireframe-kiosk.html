Feature Summary

  • Left Panel — Event Booth Branding: Dark card with J.B. Hunt logo, event name, and tagline. Same visual identity as current.
  • Left Panel — Event Info: Location, Date, Mode rows. Quick reference for the recruiter.
  • Left Panel — Form Destination Configurator: Dropdown to select intake method (Google Form, Typeform, Custom, Airtable, MS Forms). URL input field below. Recruiter pastes their form link here.
  • Left Panel — Live Status: Green pulsing dot + "Active" label + scan count. Real-time feedback on how many candidates have scanned.
  • Right Panel — QR Code: Large centered QR (240x240) with J.B. Hunt logo embedded in the center. Generated from the URL in the configurator.
  • Right Panel — Print Poster: Button to generate a print-friendly version of the QR for physical booth display.
  • Right Panel — Copy QR Link: One-click copy of the form URL to clipboard for sharing.
  • Key UX win: Kiosk is now a recruiter control station, not a public data entry form. Candidates scan and fill on their own device — better privacy, no line blocking.
